About us

We are not your typical construction company.

Lathrem Homebuilders is a licensed Arizona general contractor based in Tucson, building custom homes, remodels and outdoor living throughout Pima County.

Our approach

Decades of experience, and a quest to keep improving.

We are passionate about building and committed to delivering on our promises. Experience across every phase of the construction process, combined with practical knowledge and a genuine appreciation for a wide variety of design styles, is what lets us deliver remarkable results.

We believe that truly superior homes are built by people of high character, whose values are marked by honesty, accountability, and a dedication to doing things right the first time. Based on those principles, our goal is simple: create long-term relationships, treat every project as if it were our own, and stand behind everything we say and do.

Charles Lathrem still keeps an eye on houses this company finished years ago. That is the measure we hold ourselves to.

What we hold to

Six things we won't trade away.

01

Honesty

Straight answers about cost, schedule and what is actually possible on your site, including when the answer is one you would rather not hear.

02

Accountability

We own the outcome. If something isn't right, we fix it, during construction and long after the final walkthrough.

03

Craftsmanship

Doing it right the first time. The details you notice on the tenth visit are the ones we spend the most time on.

04

Communication

Transparent budgets, regular updates and a builder who picks up the phone. Most construction problems are really communication problems.

05

Continual improvement

Decades in, we still change how we build. New materials, better systems and a commitment to personal and technological development keep our work current.

06

Relationships

We would rather build three houses for one family over twenty years than thirty for people we never hear from again.
